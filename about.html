About Me

Jericho Collado

I love scouting out unique, camera-ready spots and sharing them with anyone chasing the perfect shot.

I started this guide because I kept getting asked "where was that taken?" every time I posted a photo. Instead of answering the same DM over and over, I figured I'd put together a running list of the spots I actually visit and shoot myself — not just places that look good in someone else's feed.

Every location here is somewhere I've personally scouted and photographed, so you're not getting recycled recommendations from a listicle. I check lighting at different times of day, note how crowded a spot usually gets, and only add places I'd genuinely send a friend to.
