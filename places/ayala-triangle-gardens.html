Ayala Triangle Gardens

Ayala Triangle Gardens is one of the few spots in Metro Manila where you can get a genuinely clean, green backdrop without leaving the middle of the city. It's a landscaped park boxed in by Ayala Avenue's skyline, so you get contrast between nature and glass towers in a single frame — something you don't find in most Manila parks.

I usually shoot here in the late afternoon, when the light softens and the office crowd starts trickling in, making the paths feel more alive without getting overcrowded. It's especially good for portraits with the tree-lined paths as a backdrop, or wider architectural shots looking straight up at the surrounding buildings.
