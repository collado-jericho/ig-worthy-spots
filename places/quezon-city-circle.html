Quezon City Circle

Quezon City Circle is one of the most laid-back places to shoot in the metro — a huge open oval loop with wide sidewalks, patches of grass, and enough breathing room that you're never fighting a crowd for your shot. It's less about a single iconic backdrop and more about the overall vibe: joggers, vendors, and the everyday rhythm of the city passing by.

I like coming here for golden hour walks, when the low light makes the wide paths glow and gives casual, candid shots a warm, cinematic feel. It's a great spot if you're after something that feels lived-in rather than staged.

Detail Info
Address Elliptical Rd, Diliman, Quezon City
Opening hours Open 24 hours (best visited 4 PM–7 PM)
Price range Free
Best time to visit Golden hour, early evening
